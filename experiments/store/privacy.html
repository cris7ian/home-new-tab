Pale Blue Dot Tab — Privacy Policy

Pale Blue Dot Tab is a Chrome extension that replaces the default new tab with a world clock, interactive globe, weather, solar times, and a Moon phase display.

Location and weather

Using device location in the extension is optional. Chrome may ask for location permission at installation because it does not allow extensions to request that permission later. The extension does not read your device location until you select Use my location inside the dashboard. It rounds your latitude and longitude to two decimal places and sends those coordinates directly to Open-Meteo to retrieve weather and timezone data. If you enable device location, the extension can repeat this on new tabs. Nearby city labels are resolved on your device. To stop device location requests, search for and select a city instead. Search text goes to Open-Meteo’s geocoding service; selected city coordinates go to its weather service.

Other requests and local storage

For the live ISS display, the extension sends requests containing timestamps to Where the ISS at?. It does not send your device coordinates to that service. Like any website they connect to, these services receive your IP address and standard request metadata. The extension stores your manually selected location, city clocks, and theme in your browser. It does not send these settings to a developer server, read your browsing history, or use analytics or advertising trackers.

Use of data

Location and search data are used only to provide the requested dashboard features. The extension does not sell personal data or use it for advertising. The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

For privacy questions, use the contact email on the Chrome Web Store listing.
